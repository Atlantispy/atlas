## Numerical, proof and causal contracts

The [method and remaining proof boundary](../workbench/plan04/README.md)
describe independently established root/i1 record and array correspondence,
observer/source/invocation binding, exact unaffected fields and zero refusal
flips. A newly dumped hash is not independent reader binding.

The approved pose projection criterion is strictly below 2^-11 of the
correctly associated same-quantity radius, with its actual source, units, norm,
refinement/history and projection context. The quaternion L2 radius and its
four-times operator error cannot be silently interchanged. No generic epsilon,
Heat/energy allowance or width-derived accuracy bound is introduced.

The historical ≤512 ULP and ≤2^-20 relative figures are diagnostics only,
never generic automatic-PASS tolerances. The published comparator explicitly
reports those diagnostics; changed arrays without an applicable independently
proved field contract remain REVIEW. This matches the execution plan
and does not tighten or relax the implemented acceptance contract.

Addendum E keeps exact total-versus-width checking before storage rounding.
Exact zero stays zero. Positive stored totals are outward, with excess strictly
less than 2^-64 of the exact total, and still fit width. Per-cell deviations
remain exact. Source-specific E/M reconstruction preserves shared logical-cell
accounting. Unknown source/schema/quantity associations remain non-accepting.

| Verdict | Exit | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| PASS | 0 | Required comparison and proof conditions satisfied |
| REVIEW | 3 | Specific unresolved comparison or proof; not acceptance |
| FAIL | 1 | Located numerical/resource/deterministic or proof/integrity defect |
| VOID-ENVIRONMENT | 4 | Evidenced external interruption |
| VOID-OBSERVER | 4 | Evidenced external observer interruption |
| VOID-SUSPECT | 4 | Unresolved stop causality or incomplete outcome |

Each VOID blocks acceptance. Process completion, a zero exit, an assertion or a
diagnostic difference alone is not a meaningful behavioural mutation detection.
The intended predicate, complete suite, error state, process finality and retained
logs must be inspected together. No automatic scientific retry is authorised.
