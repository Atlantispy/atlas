## Method and preserved contracts

The comparator checks independently supplied source, profile, producer, reader,
adoption and invocation evidence before comparing corresponding records. Root
and interval-one coverage are distinct. Identity mappings come from independently
checked reader pairs; matching bytes or a fresh hash of dumped bytes cannot
establish correspondence. The dump brackets extraction with read-only store
tokens and binds its observer entry, interpreter, arguments and environment.
Array comparison uses explicit layouts and independent row correspondence, with
bounded one-MiB chunks per operand. Missing family or row proof remains REVIEW.

Quantity contracts name actual values, units, scalar/L1/L2/Linf norms, sources,
and applicable same-quantity errors. Exact unaffected fields and zero refusal
flips remain required. The approved direct binary64 pose projection bound is
strictly less than 2^-11 of its correctly associated radius. The retained
512-ULP and 2^-20 reports are diagnostics, not substitute acceptance limits.
There is no generic epsilon and no inferred Heat or energy allowance.

For Addendum E, exact aggregate deviation must fit the existing width before
storage rounding. Exact zero stays zero. A positive stored total must be at
least the exact total, exceed it by strictly less than 2^-64 times that total,
and remain within width. Source-specific E/M reconstruction handles split M
pieces by logical cell/support rather than counting a shared bound repeatedly.
Per-cell physical deviations remain exact. Unknown producer versions or
unproved quantity associations cannot borrow these contracts.

The producer-provenance helper derives source and call-site identities from
authenticated selected bytes, reads each independently bound record once and
emits closed data-only receipts. Three W vector routes and E quaternion/operator
outputs are mandatory when applicable. Known quaternion units are dimensionless
and its error norm is L2; an operator bound of four times internal error does
not automatically certify that quaternion's comparison radius. Current reader
records do not authenticate invocation, caller units, refinement level, history,
projection and radius correspondence, so applicable results remain REVIEW even
for equal record bytes. Arbitrary observation sidecars are refused.

Located numerical, proof/integrity or deterministic resource defects remain
FAIL. Explicit external interruptions and unresolved stops retain their separate
VOID classes. Each VOID and REVIEW blocks acceptance. Assertions, diagnostic
differences and a zero process exit are not interchangeable with a behavioural
fault detection; test completion, intended predicate, errors, final process state
and retained streams must agree.

**What the catalog records.** It scans every module of the package source
inventory, test and fixture paths excepted, for:
- every call to a W or E producer;
- reads through another module's binding of a producer module or member
  (relays), including names bound to the import machinery and simple aliases;
- writes into any package module, wherever they are made;
- consequence routes: calls from other modules into producer-module functions
  that reach a pose site (`W._endpoint_volume` from the E builders and spatial
  ALE, `W.enclose_rotating_wall` from spatial ALE).

A slot can be BOUND only with the required authenticated source inventory,
reviewed caller sites present in both arms and the applicable producer and
dependency relation. Identical producer bytes alone are insufficient. Where
only the approved pose-rounding transforms differ, it is judged under that
rounding relation. W records are judged through the producer's exact restore,
writer and reader relations. E quaternion and operator-radius rows use their
source-specific outward-error proof; it does not certify downstream quantities.
A form the scan cannot
resolve (a computed name, source text run in place, a class-scope or deep alias,
a write into a package module) makes the affected slot REVIEW, never BOUND.
Non-canonical inventory keys, a package and a module claiming one import name,
and inputs too deep to parse fail input integrity.

**Array families.** Interval-one heads bind to the reader-checked array records
of the pinned producer receipt. Root heads bind through the collar snapshot, the
exterior state identity, partition record references and exact compact alias
names. A family without an independent binding is REVIEW, never PASS.
