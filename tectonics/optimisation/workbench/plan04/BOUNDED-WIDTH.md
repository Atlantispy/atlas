## Pose-rounding contract

The [16 October r2 design](https://github.com/Atlantispy/atlas/blob/1f1d78a6dd2ab3d1c533af9f9b5b79e769ce57fd/tectonics/optimisation/pending/pressure/width/DESIGN.md)
records Michael's approval of bounded dyadic pose rounding (b) and outward stored
deviation (c), conditional on no significant accuracy loss. Summed-phase history
replacement (a) and raising the 1 MiB integer cap were not selected. Historical
design/probe projections are distinct from the later bounded evidence above.

All inserted arithmetic uses exact int/Fraction and dyadic `Q(2)**k`, never
binary64 exponentiation for negative k. Output stays tuples of Fractions, keeping
exact-scale callers eligible. Ray rounding belongs in the one shared pose-vector
step loop, including inverse mode/all levels/callers. After the existing rotated
step returns vector v and actual radius increment delta, use
`q = 2**(floor_log2(min(delta, target)) - 12)` for nonzero delta, round components
to nearest q, and round the accumulated radius plus q **up** to that grid.
The original target is max input magnitude / 2**(32+2L). Euclidean movement is
at most sqrt(3)q/2 ≤ q; the extra outward radius lies in [q,2q). Orthogonal
rotation preserves additive-radius enclosure. Actual delta, rather than only
policy target, prevents over-rounding slow/very exact or swept steps.

Delta zero remains exactly unrounded, including zero-time steps: end(n) equals
start(n+1), and exact zero/coaxial special cases are preserved. The pose-binding
sum of L1 point difference plus Euclidean tail is valid as a **2-norm** bound;
a 1-norm reading would require sqrt(3) times that tail. No hidden norm change
or generic ULP allowance is introduced.

Quaternion composition retains each segment/history and error recurrence
`e_exact = (1+delta)*e + delta`. With nonzero delta, q is derived from its actual
error at guard 12. Noncoaxial histories round four components; exactly coaxial
histories round `(w,s*k)`, not each axis component independently. Choose k from
decoded nonzero rates only when every cross product is exactly zero; preserve
the exact axis ratio, rounding s on a finer grid based on ceil_log2(|k|_1).
This preserves both rotation and transpose acting exactly on k, required by
posed-boundary/pressure-axis certificates. Nonparallel histories already have
no common-axis certificate from history. Add q to the error before upward-grid
rounding, retain original `e<1` and `4e<=operator_goal` refusals, and leave zero
segments unrounded. One summed-phase quaternion was rejected because Taylor
order/width grows with phase and the recorded segment list would change.

Both guard-12 added-error bounds are <0.049% of the corresponding **actual**
certified radius/error, independent of the tested history length, versus the
design's 1% requirement. The quaternion acceptance margin cannot flip under its
existing construction proof. Ray band/sign/exact-zero tests can only newly refuse
in that narrow margin; width tests may refine further or hit their existing leaf
ceiling. Pressure force/torque/power pose terms rise by the same bound and can
newly refuse if their margin is smaller. These preserve enclosure, not universal
bit identity: coarse L≤4 binary64 projections may change low bits (design estimate
up to ~512 ULP at L0), while remaining within the much larger certified radius.

Moving-interface D1/D2/G-PY and width transforms must compare/compose on the same
reviewed base. A transform touching a kernel output anchors on that output's
actual hash; disjoint text alone does not establish source qualification. Serial/
parallel parity is still same-package parity. New source/pose/metric/ledger IDs
form an explicit successor from IV1; mixing older stores is refused. The original
replay/GO proposals are historical, not permission for new interval validation.

## Stored-deviation contract

Differential and exterior builders keep exact cell deviations and exact total
comparison against width. **After** that comparison only, store zero unchanged,
otherwise `min(round_up(total, 2**(floor_log2(total)-64)), width)`. Stored ≥ exact
total, excess <2**−64 times total and stored ≤ width. Thus the existing acceptance
decision cannot flip; nonexact/out-of-range inputs refuse instead of being rounded.
No reader may silently start treating this header as an exact sum.

The [exterior addendum](https://github.com/Atlantispy/atlas/blob/1f1d78a6dd2ab3d1c533af9f9b5b79e769ce57fd/tectonics/optimisation/pending/pressure/width/DESIGN-r2-ADDENDUM-E.md)
reviewed both native/record exterior builders. After the exact check the only read
of total_error was header encoding; mesh tables merely stored/booked it, and no
reviewed reader rederived it. Per-cell fields remain exact. The same-base r2
extension changed exterior only: 75 checks passed, four mutants caught downward
rounding, missing min and rounding before either builder's exact check. Removing
the insertion reconstructed original builder ASTs; unchanged quaternion/unit/
route/enclosure evidence was reused. Those statements concern that selected
source and must be revisited if a consumer starts computing with the header.

## Historical width population and growth

The [16 October magnitude study](https://github.com/Atlantispy/atlas/blob/1f1d78a6dd2ab3d1c533af9f9b5b79e769ce57fd/tectonics/optimisation/workbench/probes/step8/claude-20261015/heat-width/heat-magnitude-width-20261016.md)
analysed the IV1 census and unchanged source, plus a one-to-six-step pose micro-test.
It distinguishes the 614,116-bit **single mesh deviation sum** from per-element
mesh metrics (38,455-bit maximum); neither is simply a coordinate. The widest
per-cell heat residual was 2,054,539 bits (24.5% of the cap), versus 411,073-bit
per-face energy. Exact corrected enthalpy/stock-projection residual reached
1,724,160 bits. Counts/field tables and census provenance remain at that link.

Each heat call uses its interval-end metric but reprojects prior stocks/capacities/
sources/public finish data to binary64 and predictors to Decimal80. Heat arithmetic
therefore has fixed construction degree per cell/stencil rather than recursively
compounding previous exact heat. The exact metric carries the original geometry's
growing symbolic pose history: one new segment/interval, with static clip/assembly
depth. Transport likewise reprojects concentration. Whole-mesh sums also depend
on contributor count. The micro-test's approximately constant per-step width
increment supports linear growth, not multiplication by four per interval.

The old paper extrapolation put heat IV2 at 43–49%, IV3 62–73%, IV4 80–98% and
first refusal around IV5; cell/limited-face counts remain data dependent. The
rounded-route forecast (~5–7% heat, flat) was **projected**, never a substitute
for authenticated producer/reader measurements. Original r9 exact-width
reproduction applies only to its original generation; a new width identity needs
its own declared baseline. Record field-wise numerator/denominator maxima, counts
and trajectory length. Apply the unchanged gate above to actual successor tables:
completed results can survive a failed continuation gate, but the next interval
cannot proceed. No cap increase, continuation override, full C4 or run admission
follows from these historical analyses.
