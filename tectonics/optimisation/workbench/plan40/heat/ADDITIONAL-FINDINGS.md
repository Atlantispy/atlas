## Why the inverse reduction preserves the calculation

Before pivot j, left columns k<j are exactly identity columns. A pivot swap
within rows j onwards preserves those columns. Normalising the pivot row divides
their zeros by a finite nonzero pivot, yielding exact zeros. Eliminating another
row computes x−value*0 in those columns, where x is zero or one. Those operations
are exact, finite and introduce no rounding/range/invalid flags under the
original trapped contexts. This proves the identity invariant by induction.

Later pivots read only their own trailing column; the returned value contains only
the right half. Earlier left entries never become operands in retained arithmetic.
Their discarded zero signs may differ, which is permissible only because they
remain unread. This does not license skipping a zero term in a returned dot or
retaining a stale ternary result. A nonfinite operation on retained entries still
traps at the same operation after the skipped exact work. All cancellation/phase
checks remain. For rank n, omitted normalisation divisions are n(n−1)/2 and
omitted elimination multiplies/subtracts are each n(n−1)²/2.

## Additional rejected shortcuts and next action

Keep the original off-diagonal Decimal +0 operation: it can change representation
and flags even after a rounded product. Sharing its zero operand is different.
Do not replace MPFR division by multiplication with a rounded reciprocal, skip
residual checks, trust boundary signatures instead of their original reads, or
merge exact and rounded public energies. Shared support coordinates do not justify
shared face rankings, factors or certificates. Conductivity/beta cancellation
would change intermediates and guard history and is not selected here.

Decimal digit-coefficient and generic factor-conversion caching were inspected
but do not supply another demonstrated repeated unchanged-input population worth
a new retained cache. Keep the existing bounded converter; no global context,
rounded-output cache or extra optimisation project is introduced. C20 is limited
to the explicit immutable constructor/sign cases proved above.
