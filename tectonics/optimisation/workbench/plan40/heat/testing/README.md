## Completed component measurements

The controlled workload independently compared each candidate with its unchanged
reference. Three serial groups used one process/core at a time under an exclusive
five-minute, 1 GiB booking and the required live physical-memory guards. The
successful payload took about six seconds. Its booking is not a production limit.

| Solve/finish | Eight candidates; three alternating-order samples per arm, three complete adapter calls per sample | Tiny actual consumer bodies with declared synthetic seams; C10 includes its entire search |

The same entry points pass untimed smoke checks. Missing arms, non-PASS status,
source drift and child failures cannot produce an aggregate PASS. Assertions are
required: all entries refuse optimised Python before numerical work. The
comparison retains raw samples, workload/output digests and declared ownership
costs. Instrumentation and signature construction inside component calls can
affect the result and must remain disclosed.

These inputs are synthetic. Measurements determine whether these particular
component prototypes merit further work; they cannot establish production hit
rates, whole-IV benefit or a native memory theorem. Do not add overlapping C2/C14
or C16/C17 savings. No environment, frozen helper, method, resource guard, H5a
disposition or scientific hold is changed.

The larger operation-count cuts did not consistently reduce elapsed time.
Bounded additive source work follows the C3/C8 component results, with a small
C13 trial preserving the existing owner. Actual source generations, including
their new loader and source checks, need their own affected qualification;
prototype percentages do not automatically describe an implemented receiver.
