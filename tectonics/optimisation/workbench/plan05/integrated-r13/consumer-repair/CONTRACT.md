# Held successor consumer contract

The proof gate and plan adapter preserve q10 FAIL and q22 HELD.
They do not create acceptance, run authority or queue release. No real accepted
or authorised record accompanies this public source snapshot.

`validate_selection(path, sha256)` validates exact source/evidence bindings,
the complete repaired-pair result, process/log finality, every required metadata
disposition, explicit scientific correctness acceptance and separate q22
authority. `validate_child` additionally binds mode, layout, index and output.
`build_plan` refuses an existing output and returns commands/environments; it
does not dispatch. The owned launcher exclusively claims the output, supervises
children, preserves failures and observes finality.

The fixed schedule is `time lw ld3 le3 lf3 lh lh lf3 le3 ld3 lw`, then `prof lh`.
The repair applies only to lf3/lh. Children retain seed zero, `-B -s -P`, the
selected native clone and six workers. Only the profiling arm adds its pinned
profile hook and destination. Proof-only and consumer worker checks are separate.

The consumer selection binds the original proved selection, its five repair
files, complete proof artefacts, five native layouts, interpreter, launcher,
writer, verifier, consumer helpers, queue/checker/backend and profiling hook.
Prediction history consists of exactly the two original pinned historical
summaries. Proof captures cannot stand in for timing/profile measurements.
Before each arm the route checks bindings and predicts resources; queue booking
must cover the prediction. Actual memory/CPU observations and bounded waits
remain the launcher's responsibility. Distinct worker PID count remains null
where native receipts do not provide it.

A valid repaired comparison must separately show equal physical inputs,
complete cell/face/orientation maps, all five bitwise arrays, four exact account
families, face/scalar accounts and both native ledgers. Unknown required
evidence, invariant differences and codec-proof failures refuse. Metadata
decisions cannot waive numerical failures. Historical `NOT_REPRODUCED` and
the original nonzero verifier result remain visible.

Owner dispositions bind each exact required entry and a reason; they do not
assert equivalence or acceptance. The separate correctness acceptance binds
selection, verification, dispositions and complete proof artefacts to actual
authority evidence. Run authority binds that acceptance, exact source-pin map,
schedule and output to separate authority evidence. These are machine-checked
references to supplied decisions, not authentication of the human decision.
Native queue release/admission is an additional requirement.

Missing acceptance or run authority is an expected held state and refuses every
child. Synthetic positive/fault fixtures create explicitly synthetic authority
only within disposable tests. They do not authorise a scientific run.
