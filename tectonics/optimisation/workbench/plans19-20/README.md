## Accepted outcomes and remaining work

Plan19's implementation disposition and bounded native validation are accepted.
The retained shell/axis complete-call cases improved by 7.40% and 18.44%, with
explicit work/memory trade-offs. Three other tested candidates remain excluded.

Plan20 A/B is accepted. Its retained three-capture comparison used 48.9264% less
median write time and 13.4949% less disk. It excludes decoding, block preparation
and initial DLL loading, and establishes no RAM or full-interval saving. The
folded DLL binding, its module-lookup failure controls, and actual prepared-entry
execution remain outstanding; the Plan20 issue stays open. Known base consumer
failures and the dictionary skip are not described as passing coverage.

The per-plan method records contain the original receipt identities and limits.
Performance ratios have different or overlapping scopes and are not combined.
No scientific run, main-package/default adoption, hold release or scientific
acceptance follows from publication. Reuse in another Atlas module requires the
same geometry/data/resource contracts and that module's own integration checks.
