## Implementation and independent controls

`src/atlas_tectonics/column_loads.py` implements immutable support/catalogue/state
inputs and four-account loads. `reuse.cached_column_load_change` uses the existing
verified storage/admission/runtime path; public constructors/kernel are exported.
SHA256 of the load module:
`efaa52cda1c59937183b5956c08c05df0bec26e40307b67e9ba998459b34c067`.

Independent tests use arbitrary synthetic SI values, not inferred Earth inputs:

- Compaction with matching replacement water: inventory sheet -0.8 kg/m2,
  replacement +0.8 kg/m2, zero total pressure.
- Same compaction with negligible-density air: -1.6 Pa unloading.
- One unit of grain deposition replacing water: +2.4 Pa; erosion reverses it.
- Unchanged material with changed density in the finite unoccupied gap: +3.2 Pa.
  Changing replacement density under a completely occupied support has no effect.
- Cooling a fixed three-metre thermal region by five kelvin: +0.24 Pa, no
  inventory change. Warming reverses it; disjoint opposite anomalies cancel.
- Independent absolute supported-mass calculations, reference zero, reversal,
  additivity, horizontal/phase subdivision, and compensated opposing large loads.
- Refusals for mismatched support/catalogue, invalid capacity/shape/temperature,
  changed thermal volume, corrected-density double counting, over/underflow;
  immutable inputs/descriptors, source identity, memory admission and cancellation.
- Cache auto-bypass, exact restored outputs, and invalidation for source, volume
  and gravity changes. Existing execution/source identity tests remain passing.
